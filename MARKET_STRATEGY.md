# MajiSmart Kenya Go-to-Market (2026)

## Problem worth paying for
NRW 48%, KES 13.7–15.7B lost above 25% benchmark. Collection efficiency 94% where digitized, 35–40% on cash kiosks. Rationing is normal (Nairobi 2–4 days/week zones; Coast deficit 327,000 m3/day). Counties are benchmarked publicly (WASREB top/bottom 10) — shame + donor PIAP pressure sells.

## Beachhead (0–90d): estates
- Where: Ruiru, Ruaka, Syokimau, Ngong, Nakuru — 50 estates via property managers + Davis & Shirtliff installers.
- Offer: "Zero arrears in 60 days or 3 months SaaS free."
- Price: HW 11.5–14.5k, SaaS KES 150/meter/mo or 3.5% (min 3k/estate/mo), install 1.5k, activation 100.
- Example: 40 units × 12.5k = 500k HW + 6k/mo SaaS. Landlord payback <4 months.

## WSP wedge (90–180d): 2 county WSPs
- Targets: Nanyuki, Embu, Kikuyu, Ruiru-Juja (NRW 35–55%).
- Offer: free NRW audit from WARIS + 1 DMA paid pilot KES 350k/90d, credited on annual.
- Price: Starter 45k/mo/5k conns; NRW Pro 120–250k/mo/DMA + 15% gain-share 12mo (capped).

## Scale (180–365d): coast + rural
- Mombasa/Malindi vendor formalization (2025 Regs) + Mwache Dam readiness.
- 20 ATMs Kitui/Makueni with NGO (eWATER playbook: 16 villages, 50k people yr1).

## Why we win
- Hybrid: WSP SaaS + landlord prepaid + rural ATM in one cloud (Wonderkid=software only, Dayliff=hardware only).
- WASREB-ready: 9 KPIs, DMA balance, vendor permits, tariff engine.
- Offline-first + M-Pesa native: keypad + LoRa auto-load, token recovery, SMS fallback, *384*99#.
- Gain-share: sell recovered revenue, not meters.

## Competition
